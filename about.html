About ZenAS Power

An engineering solutions and trading company in Chattogram, keeping buildings across Bangladesh powered, safe and connected.

Who we are

ZenAS Power is an engineering solutions and trading company based in Chattogram, Bangladesh. We bring together two things that are usually handled by separate companies: the supply of quality power, electrical and safety equipment, and the engineering expertise to design, install and maintain it. Our work covers twelve service lines, from rooftop, hybrid and off-grid solar, substations, generators and street lighting to lightning protection, fire safety, CCTV, lifts, automation, house wiring and smart home solutions. Whether you need a single rooftop system for your home or a complete power, safety and security package for a large building, we give you one accountable team, fewer contractors and clearer responsibility.

We import equipment from trusted international manufacturers and supply leading brands such as Jinko Solar, LONGi, Trina Solar, Huawei and Sungrow, so our clients receive genuine products at competitive prices. Contractors, dealers, developers and project buyers can purchase from us in wholesale quantities, while clients who need a complete solution can rely on our team from the first site visit to final commissioning. We also supply products to buyers outside Bangladesh, with reliable documentation and delivery.

Our clients include government bodies such as PWD, LGED, DPHE, WASA and BADC, city corporations and municipalities, as well as private developers, industrial facilities, commercial establishments and homeowners. Every project follows the same careful process: we listen, survey the site, design the system, provide a clear itemised quotation, install to recognised safety standards and test everything before handover.

Our commitment continues after a project is complete. We offer maintenance contracts, fast response to breakdowns and upgrades as needs grow, so solar systems, lifts, generators and safety systems keep performing at their best. Our vision is to become Bangladesh's most trusted partner for power, safety and smart building solutions, guided by safety, honest advice, quality workmanship and on-time delivery.
